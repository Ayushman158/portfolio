Choose
Assam

After a flood, Tata Tea points its packs and ads at the makers of Assam, so people can buy from them instead of donating.

Client
Tata Tea Premium
Scope
Campaign · Packaging · Maker platform
Context
M.Des, UPES · 2026, speculative

Problem

Floods close workshops in Assam almost every monsoon. The news moves on in days; reopening takes months. Donations help once, then the link to the maker ends.

What I did

A campaign system for Tata Tea Premium: six community pack editions, a QR on every surface, and a maker platform where each maker’s status decides how people can help.

How it was made

I wrote the brief and the system, designed the pack system and built the platform. The photographs, patterns and film were generated with AI, and I art-directed them.

Status

Speculative and untested. Success would be measured by repeat orders per maker, and how many makers reach Open within 90 days.

The idea

Not a donation. An order.

The news moves on in days. Reopening takes months. An order keeps a maker going; a buyer can come back.

Tradeoffs

What I chose, and what it cost.

ConstraintWhat I choseWhat it cost
A brand shouldn’t look like it profits from a flood.Buying, pre-ordering and lending. No donate button.Harder to explain than “donate”, and it only helps makers who can sell again.
Packs can’t be reprinted every week.One QR on every pack. What changes lives on the platform.It depends on the collective keeping statuses up to date.
Using community crafts on a commercial pack.Name each community on the front, cite the craft on the back, link to its makers.The patterns would have to be made with the communities and paid for before print.

Project notes

How the system works

Every pack and ad carries the same QR. It opens the maker platform, where each maker’s status decides what you can do. A maker collective checks every maker and posts their updates.

Reach · Tata Tea’s media

  • Film60 seconds, told once at scale
  • SocialMaker updates, every week
  • PackIn the kitchen every morning
  • ShelfEnd-caps in stores
  • OutdoorBillboards that name a maker

Bridge

  • One QRSame code on every surface, one destination

Platform · the microsite

  • BuyFrom makers who are open
  • Pre-orderPay now, goods later; funds working capital
  • Lend or backThrough regulated partners

Rules

  • Status engineClosed to Open; sets which money can reach a maker
  • Trust ledgerPublic proof for every maker

Ground

  • Maker collectiveVerifies makers, posts updates
  • MakersCraft, food, homestays

Backing makers, legally

Equity crowdfunding isn’t legal in India, so the platform uses pre-orders, a licensed lending partner and the Social Stock Exchange.

RouteWhoHow it worksLegal basis
Pre-orderAnyonePay now, receive the goods later. It funds the maker’s working capital.Reward crowdfunding, legal without sector-specific rules (InCorpX)
LendIndividualsSmall loans through a licensed P2P partner. Rang De is the model.RBI NBFC-P2P licence; escrow is mandatory and no repayment guarantee is allowed (Rang De, IES)
Back the collectiveTata and institutionsSubscribe to zero-coupon zero-principal (ZCZP) instruments of a partner NPO listed on the Social Stock Exchange.Since May 2026, up to 10% of a company’s CSR obligation can go to ZCZPs (S.S. Rana)
