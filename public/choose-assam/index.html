Project notes

How the system works

Every pack and ad carries the same QR. It opens the maker platform, where each maker’s status decides what you can do. A maker collective checks every maker and posts their updates.

Backing makers, legally

Equity crowdfunding isn’t legal in India, so the platform uses pre-orders, a licensed lending partner and the Social Stock Exchange.

RouteWhoHow it worksLegal basis
Pre-orderAnyonePay now, receive the goods later. It funds the maker’s working capital.Reward crowdfunding, legal without sector-specific rules (InCorpX)
LendIndividualsSmall loans through a licensed P2P partner. Rang De is the model.RBI NBFC-P2P licence; escrow is mandatory and no repayment guarantee is allowed (Rang De, IES)
Back the collectiveTata and institutionsSubscribe to zero-coupon zero-principal (ZCZP) instruments of a partner NPO listed on the Social Stock Exchange.Since May 2026, up to 10% of a company’s CSR obligation can go to ZCZPs (S.S. Rana)

Backing the collective gives Tata a regulated, auditable way to anchor the fund, a stronger story than “we donated”.

Trust

  1. 01Verification. The collective checks every maker in person before listing. Each listing shows their Udyam registration and any GI tags.
  2. 02Milestone release. Money unlocks by status, as above. Nothing reaches a stage the maker hasn’t reached.
  3. 03A public ledger per maker. Orders fulfilled, money received and used, repayments made, and photo updates at 30, 60 and 90 days.
  4. 04Risk labels and a grievance path on every loan, because the platform can’t guarantee repayment.

Tradeoffs and what’s still open
ConstraintWhat I choseWhat it cost
A brand shouldn’t look like it profits from a flood.Buying, pre-ordering and lending. No donate button.Harder to explain than “donate”, and it only helps makers who can sell again.
Makers recover at different speeds.A status for every maker that decides what you can do.In the first weeks most makers aren’t open, so lending and pre-orders carry the load.
Packs can’t be reprinted every week.One QR on every pack. What changes lives on the platform.It depends on the collective keeping statuses up to date.
Using community crafts on a commercial pack.Name each community on the front, cite the craft on the back, link to its makers.The patterns would need to be made with the communities and paid for before print.

Still open

  1. 01The patterns and portraits are generated. A real edition would be drawn with each community and checked by them.
  2. 02The Majuli pattern needs checking against real satra masks.
  3. 03Real makers on the platform, in place of the illustrative ones.
  4. 04Earlier campaigns did parts of this. Tata Tea’s own Desh Ke Dhaage packs featured handloom weavers, and #KeralaIsOpen brought visitors back after Kerala’s 2018 floods. Choose Assam joins the pack to a live list of makers.
