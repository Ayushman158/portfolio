      How it works

      1. 01Buy the pack

        The Choose Assam edition of Tata Tea Premium, in stores and on quick-commerce apps.

      2. 02Scan the QR on the pouch

        The same code is on the shelf and on billboards. It always brings you here.

      3. 03See where a maker is

        Closed, rebuilding, producing or open. Statuses are updated every week.

      4. 04Buy, pre-order or lend

        The maker’s status decides which. Nothing is a donation.

        Why you can trust it

        1. 01Verified in person

          The Choose Assam Maker Collective visits every maker before listing them and checks their Udyam registration and any GI tag.

        2. 02Money unlocks by status

          Repair funds while rebuilding, working capital while producing. Nothing reaches a stage the maker hasn’t reached.

        3. 03A public ledger

          Every maker page shows orders fulfilled, money received and used, repayments, and photo updates at 30, 60 and 90 days.

        4. 04Loans are labelled

          Lending runs through a licensed P2P partner with escrow. Repayment can’t be guaranteed, and every loan says so.

        For companies. Back the collective through zero-coupon zero-principal instruments on the Social Stock Exchange. Up to 10% of a CSR obligation can go to them.
