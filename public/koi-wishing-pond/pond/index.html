Koi Wishing Pond

The installation from UPES, running in your browser: the same koi, water and wish.

  1. 01Move over the water. Ripples follow and the koi drift over to see.
  2. 02Press and hold for a moment to close your hand around a wish.
  3. 03Let go. The coin drops and the koi gather around it.

With a webcam you can use your hand instead, as visitors did. Nothing leaves your device.

Your browser can’t run the live pond, so here it is recorded.
